## 🎯 How to Use the Detector  

Using the tool is as simple as 1-2-3:  

1. **Type or paste your text** into the large text area.  
2. **Click the "Detect" button** (or press Enter).  
3. **Read your result**—you'll see a score from 0 to 100.  

- **Score 0–30:** Likely written by a human.  
- **Score 31–60:** Unclear—could be either.  
- **Score 61–100:** Likely generated by AI.  

The score is a *probability estimate*. It tells you how confident the model is that the text is AI-generated. It's a useful guide, but it's not absolute proof.

## ⚙️ What's Under the Hood?  

This tool is built using a **machine learning model** that's been trained on thousands of examples of human-written and AI-written text. Here's what happens behind the scenes:  

1. **The app breaks your text into smaller pieces** (called tokens).  
2. **It analyzes patterns** like word choice, sentence length, and complexity.  
3. **It compares those patterns** to what it learned during training.  
4. **It assigns a score** based on how closely the text matches AI writing styles.  

Everything runs locally on your computer—no internet connection is needed once you've downloaded the files. Your text stays private and is never uploaded anywhere.  

---

## 📖 Understanding the Score  

It's important to know that **this score is not a judgment**. It's a statistical measurement based on patterns. Here are a few things to keep in mind:  

- **A high score doesn't mean "bad."** It just means the text has characteristics common in AI writing.  
- **A low score doesn't guarantee human authorship.** Some AI models are very good at mimicking human writing.  
- **The score is based on the training data.** The model learned from a specific set of examples, so its judgment is shaped by those examples.  

Use the score as a *starting point* for your own evaluation, not as a final verdict.
